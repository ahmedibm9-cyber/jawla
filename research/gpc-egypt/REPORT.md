## Business Model

GPC is a **trading/distribution intermediary**, not a manufacturer:

1. **Imports** raw plastic materials (virgin polymers) and chemicals from major producers in the **Middle East**
2. **Distributes** these materials to Egyptian manufacturers and industrial clients
3. **Provides logistics** support — shipping, customs clearance, delivery
4. **Exports** materials to regional markets
