## Business Model

GPC operates as a **trading and distribution intermediary** — not a manufacturer. The company:

1. **Imports** raw plastic materials (virgin polymers) and chemicals from major producers in the **Middle East**
2. **Distributes** these materials to Egyptian manufacturers and industrial clients
3. **Provides logistics** support including shipping, customs clearance, and delivery
4. **Exports** materials to regional markets

**Key Differentiator:** Partnerships with the best sources of raw plastic production in the Middle East, combined with comprehensive logistics capabilities.
