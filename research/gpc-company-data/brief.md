# Research Brief: GPC (Global Plastic Company) — Real-World Data Validation

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Depth Mode:** quick (limited budget, focused research)

## Core Question

Validate that the demo data in `DemoSeeder` reflects the real Egyptian plastics/polymers trading industry — specifically for GPC (شركة اللدائن العالمية), a fictional but realistic company modeled after real Egyptian polymer trading firms.

## Scope

### In scope

- Verify product names, categories, SKUs match real Egyptian market
- Verify supplier names (SABIC, Borouge, ExxonMobil, Golden Power) are realistic
- Verify pricing ranges for polymers in EGP/ton
- Verify customer types (industrial, commercial, packaging) match real industry
- Verify Egyptian geographic regions (Cairo/Obour, Giza/6 October, Alexandria/Borg El Arab) match industrial zones

### Out of scope

- GPC is fictional — no need to find the exact company
- Financial data accuracy (invoices, payments) — these are randomized
- Individual customer names — generic is fine

## Angles

1. **Egyptian polymer/plastic market overview** — What products trade in Egypt? What are common polymers/chemicals?
2. **Pricing data** — What are realistic EGP prices per ton for PP, PE, PVC, PET, PS, PC, chemicals, packaging?
3. **Supplier landscape** — Who supplies Egyptian plastics companies? Are SABIC, Borouge, ExxonMobil real players?
4. **Industrial zones & customer types** — Where are Egyptian plastics factories located? What are typical customer segments?
