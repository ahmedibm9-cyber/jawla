# Story CG5.3 -- Rep Target Progress and Reports

**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Epic:** CG5 -- Sales Targets & Attainment
**Estimated effort:** Small/Medium (~3-4 days)
**Blocked by:** CG5.2
**Labels:** targets, rep-ui, reports, p2

---

## Story

**As a** rep  
**I want** to see my current target progress in the app  
**So that** I understand how close I am to my goals today and this month.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Rep home shows target progress for the active period.
- Progress updates from real persisted activity, not optimistic local state.
- Reports page exposes target attainment in Arabic and English.
- Empty-state copy exists when no target is assigned.
