# Story CG3.3 -- Location Privacy and Battery Controls

**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Epic:** CG3 -- Live Rep Tracking
**Estimated effort:** Small/Medium (~2-3 days)
**Blocked by:** CG3.1
**Labels:** gps, privacy, battery, p1

---

## Story

**As a** rep and company admin  
**I want** clear controls and policy around live tracking  
**So that** the feature feels fair, transparent, and battery-safe.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Tracking starts only inside active work sessions.
- Rep-facing copy explains when tracking is active.
- Company-level cadence / enablement settings exist.
- Battery/performance impact is measured on target devices.
- Privacy policy and in-app messaging are updated accordingly.
