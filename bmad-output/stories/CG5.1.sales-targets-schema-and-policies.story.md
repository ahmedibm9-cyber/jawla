**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Epic:** CG5 -- Sales Targets & Attainment
**Estimated effort:** Small/Medium (~2-3 days)
**Blocked by:** none

## Story

**As a** manager  
**I want** target entities and access controls defined  
**So that** attainment can be set and viewed safely by role.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Schema exists for per-rep targets by period and metric.
- Metrics supported at MVP: sales value, collections value, visit count.
- Only allowed roles can create/update targets.
- Tenant isolation and audit logging apply.
