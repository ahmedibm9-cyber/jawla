# Story CG1.3 -- Rep Print Workflow and Device Certification

**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Epic:** CG1 -- Portable Field Printing
**Estimated effort:** Small (~1 day)
**Blocked by:** CG1.1, CG1.2
**Labels:** printing, ux, qa, p1

---

## Story

**As a** release owner  
**I want** the print workflow fully integrated and field-tested  
**So that** printing is trustworthy in real sales demos and pilot deployments.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Print button appears on the right success states only.
- Device/printer test matrix documented with pass/fail results.
- Browser/E2E or manual certification checklist exists for print flow.
- Rep can reprint last document without recreating the transaction.
- Failures are logged without blocking the already-saved transaction.
