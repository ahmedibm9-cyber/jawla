# Story CG1.2 -- Thermal Receipt and Invoice Templates

**Status:** ready-for-dev
**Epic:** CG1 -- Portable Field Printing
**Estimated effort:** Small/Medium (~1-2 days)
**Blocked by:** CG1.1
**Labels:** printing, template, pdf, p1

---

## Story

**As a** field rep  
**I want** printed output optimized for thermal paper  
**So that** invoices and receipts are legible, branded, and compact on portable printers.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- Thermal templates exist for invoice and payment receipt.
- Arabic and English text render correctly on narrow paper widths.
- Totals, QR code, signature reference, company name, customer name, date, and document number are included.
- Long product names wrap safely without truncating totals.
- Printed layout is validated on at least two common printer widths.
