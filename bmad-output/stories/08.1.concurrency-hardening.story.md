## Story

**As the** business owner,
**I want** every money/stock mutation to be impossible to apply twice and every sale to be backed by real stock,
**so that** double-taps, retries, and two people acting at once can never corrupt inventory, cash, or customer balances.

---

## Context (from the investigation — do not rediscover)

- The correct locking idiom **already exists in this repo**: `CashReconciliationService::review()` (CashReconciliationService.php:56) — `lockForUpdate()` re-fetch inside the transaction + status re-check + `throw_if`. Copy that shape; do not invent a new one.
- `SalesFlow::submit()` (SalesFlow.php:182-241) never resets `$cart`/`$customerId` after success — a queued second Livewire call passes all validations and creates a second invoice (E1). `CollectPayment::submit()` line 97 shows the correct reset pattern.
- None of the cancel paths check "already cancelled": `InvoiceService::cancelWithoutTransaction()` (InvoiceService.php:198-232), `ReturnService::cancel()` (:72), `PaymentService::cancel()` (:58), `ExpenseService::cancel()` (:35). `ActionToast` (ActionToast.php:81-103) pre-checks `whereNull('cancelled_at')` **outside** the transaction — that guard is decorative under concurrency.
- `VanTransferService` `ship/receive/cancel/reject` (VanTransferService.php:43-134) check status after a plain `findOrFail()` — no lock.
- `PaymentService::collect()` (:13-56) has no invoice-status guard and no overpayment handling; `remaining_amount` can go negative.
- `InvoiceService::create()` (:106-110) blindly marks a proforma converted; `StockService::move()` (:84-91) can double-create the stock row on first sale (partial unique index kills one txn as a 500); `StockService::reconcile()` (:47-73) reads and writes without any lock.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Sale double-submit dead:** `SalesFlow::submit()` gains (a) an early-return guard when `step !== 'cart'`, (b) full reset of `cart`/`customerId` state on success. Pest test: two consecutive `submit()` calls → exactly one invoice, one set of stock movements, one balance increment.
2. **Cancel-once, everywhere:** `InvoiceService`, `ReturnService`, `PaymentService`, `ExpenseService` cancel paths and all four `VanTransferService` transitions re-fetch with `lockForUpdate()` inside the transaction and `throw_if` the status/`cancelled_at` no longer permits the action (mirror `CashReconciliationService::review()`). Pest tests per service: second cancel throws AND stock/cash/balance are byte-identical to after the first cancel. Van transfer: double-`ship()` moves stock exactly once.
3. **Stale-read cancel fixed:** `InvoiceService` cancel locks the invoice row before reading `paid_amount`, so a concurrent payment cannot skew the balance reversal.
4. **Payments guarded:** `collect()` rejects payments against cancelled/draft invoices (bilingual error). Overpayment policy (owner decision): amount up to `remaining_amount` applies to the invoice; any surplus does NOT drive `remaining_amount` negative — invoice caps at fully paid, full amount still credits the customer balance (surplus = customer credit). Tests: exact-pay, overpay, pay-on-cancelled.
5. **Stock-backed sales always:** the `if ($vanWarehouse)` silent skip in `InvoiceService` (create + submit + cancel) and `ReturnService` is removed. A rep with a van decrements the van; a rep with **no van warehouse is blocked from selling** with a bilingual error ("مخزون السيارة غير مُعد — تواصل مع مديرك / Your van stock is not set up — contact your manager"). Same for returns. _(Owner said sales follow warehouse availability; blocking the unconfigured-rep case is the assumption encoded here — flagged in Dev Notes.)_
6. **Proforma converts once:** conversion uses a conditional update (`where status != converted_to_invoice`) and throws a bilingual error when 0 rows are affected. Test: two conversions of one proforma → one invoice.
7. **First-sale stock-row race handled:** `Stock::create` inside `move()` catches `UniqueConstraintViolationException` and re-fetches with `lockForUpdate()` (same fallback shape as `NumberSequenceService::generate()`), so the loser retries instead of 500ing. `reconcile()` acquires `lockForUpdate()` on the stock row before computing the difference.
8. Full suite green; new tests cover every failure path above (CLAUDE.md: failure-path tests are mandatory for money/stock flows).
