Jawla — Competitive Gaps (vs market)

- No AI-powered features (PeppyBooks: AI order recs, HAL: AI reporting)
- No native mobile app (most competitors have Android/iOS)
- No route optimization (BizModo, Delta, Dynamics Mobile)
- No offline mode (critical for field work in low-connectivity areas)
- No WhatsApp integration (HAL ERP's key differentiator)
- Narrower ERP scope (no HR/payroll, manufacturing, procurement depth)
